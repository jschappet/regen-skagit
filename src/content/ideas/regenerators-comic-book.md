## Idea

We write the stories of regenerators around the valley. Showing them everyday super heros making the Valley a Place of Refuge.

## Why It Matters

Regeneration is not about building better habits or fixing the environment. Regeneration is about how do we live and work in attunement with this place. We are building a new culture. We want to highlight the people who are doing the work!

## What Could Happen Next

We could collect stories of the regenerators you see working around you. We could sketch out some ideas for style and art work

graphic artists, story tellers
